# Picture World Maker v0.2.3 - Release notes

**Status: RELEASED 2026-11-06.**

## QGIS is gone

Jim: *"arcgis earth can open geotiff files directly. if that is all there is lets take qgis completely out."*

Picture World Maker now does one thing: turn pictures into maps you can fly into. It needs **Python** and the free **Pillow** helper (the .bat installs it) - no QGIS.

- **Removed:** the QGIS route and everything behind it - QGIS discovery and version check, the "close QGIS" check, the staging GeoTIFF, the headless QGIS worker (`PWM_QGIS_WORKER.py`) and the QGIS settings.
- **Real map files** (GeoTIFFs with coordinates): open them directly in ArcGIS Earth. If you choose one in Picture World Maker anyway, it is built as a picture parked on the ocean, and the window says it has map coordinates.
- **Unchanged:** the photo tiler and the TPKX converter. A Kings Reach panorama build is byte-identical to v0.2.2 in all 30 bundles (35,216 tiles).

After Extract All the folder still holds just `Start Picture World Maker.bat` and `System Files`.
