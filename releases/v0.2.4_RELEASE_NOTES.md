# Picture World Maker v0.2.4 - Release notes

**Status: RELEASED 2026-11-06 - LIVE-PROVEN by Jim the same evening** (London panorama on spot 2, 53 s, 35,205 tiles, 25 bundles, flown beside the Tower of Babel on spot 1).

## World spots: a gallery on the ocean

Jim, after flying into Bruegel's *Tower of Babel*: *"We need to add multiple places on the ocean that these virtual worlds could be on. Each could have its own grid box."*

- **World spot (1-20)** in the window: each picture can be parked in its own one-degree box of open Atlantic east of Florida (30-31 N, 80-71 W), so many picture worlds sit side by side.
- **Spot 1 is the old parking spot** (30 N, 80 W). A spot-1 build is identical to v0.2.3 - all 30 bundles of the Kings Reach panorama match byte for byte - so older files stay where they are.
- **File names say where they live:** the World spot is added to the name, e.g. `Tower_of_Babel_spot02.tpkx`.
- **Too big for its box?** The window warns and asks before building.
- **Gates:** the manual lists every spot's coordinates for World Gate Maker (ground elev 0) - one gate per picture makes a gallery tour.

After Extract All the folder still holds just `Start Picture World Maker.bat` and `System Files`.
